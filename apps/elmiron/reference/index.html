Understanding the Condition

What is IC / Bladder Pain Syndrome?

Interstitial Cystitis (IC), also known as Bladder Pain Syndrome (BPS), is a chronic condition causing bladder pressure, bladder pain, and sometimes pelvic pain. The pain ranges from mild discomfort to severe, significantly impacting quality of life.

The condition predominantly affects women and is characterized by a defective GAG (glycosaminoglycan) layer that normally protects the bladder lining. When compromised, urinary substances penetrate the bladder wall causing pain and urgency.

Bladder Layer Compromise

  • 1

    GAG Layer (Glycosaminoglycan)

    Protective mucus layer lining the bladder — defective in IC/BPS patients, allowing irritants to penetrate.

  • 2

    Urothelium

    Transitional epithelial cells exposed to urinary irritants when GAG layer is compromised.

  • 3

    Suburothelial Layer

    Nerve endings and mast cells activated, triggering pain, urgency, and frequency signals.

  • 4

    Detrusor Muscle

    Chronic inflammation can lead to fibrosis and reduced bladder capacity over time.

Mechanism of Action

How Elmiron Works

Elmiron (Pentosan Polysulfate Sodium) works through a targeted, multi-step mechanism to restore bladder health and provide lasting symptomatic relief.

01

Supplements the Natural Defective Layer

Pentosan Polysulfate Sodium mimics the natural GAG layer, providing a synthetic protective coating to the compromised bladder epithelium, filling gaps in the defective mucus layer.

02

Adheres to Bladder Surface

The negatively charged polysulfate molecule has a high affinity for the bladder urothelium, allowing it to bind and coat the exposed epithelial cells, reducing penetration of urinary irritants.

03

Replenishes the Defective GAG Layer

Over time, Elmiron replenishes and restores the glycosaminoglycan layer, rebuilding the natural barrier function and reducing the hypersensitivity responses that cause pain, urgency, and frequency.

Clinical Applications

Also Effective In

Beyond IC/BPS, Elmiron demonstrates efficacy across a range of bladder conditions where the GAG layer is compromised or urothelial damage is involved.

Recurrent UTI

Restoration of GAG layer helps reduce frequency of recurrent urinary tract infections.

Hemorrhagic Cystitis

Supports mucosal repair in hemorrhagic cystitis, reducing inflammation and bleeding.

Radiation Cystitis

Palliative management of radiation-induced bladder damage and chronic cystitis symptoms.

BCG Induced Cystitis

Adjunctive management of cystitis following BCG immunotherapy for bladder cancer.

Cyclophosphamide Induced Cystitis

Bladder protection and repair following cyclophosphamide chemotherapy-induced damage.

Neurogenic Bladder

Mucosal support in neurogenic bladder conditions where normal barrier function is impaired.
